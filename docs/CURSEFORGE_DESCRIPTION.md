# GearPilot – your leveling companion for WoW Forever

**Which item makes you stronger, where do you get it – and which quests are worth your time?** GearPilot checks quest rewards, dungeon drops, vendors, the auction house, crafted gear and your bags, scores every item for your class and talents, plans your quests and dungeons and shows you the way – on its own map, with waypoints that move on by themselves.

GearPilot only reads and points the way – it never equips, buys or picks anything for you. German and English. Same look as **CraftPilot**, and both work together.

**Community, questions & bug reports:** [Discord](https://discord.gg/DpsBkHtQS) – no GitHub account needed.

## Quest route

- **The quests worth most at your level** – experience plus the upgrade value of their rewards for *your* gear. A reward at the end of a chain already counts for its first quest.
- **Focus:** experience, gear or balanced.
- **The zone that pays most for the way there**, with the nearest flight point of your faction – and the zone after it.
- **In order:** accept, objectives, turn in – nearest first. **Go** sets the waypoint and moves on by itself when you accept, complete or turn in a quest or reach an objective.
- **Follows you:** after a level up, quest changes and zone changes the route is worked out again; when a zone runs dry, the next one comes.
- Shows how far it gets you: *"enough for level 41 + 20 %"*.

## Upgrades

- **Best upgrades** from bags, quests and dungeons together, sorted by score gain – vendors, auction house and crafting can be added.
- **Quests** as items or **by zone**, sorted by score, distance or zone. The details show the **whole quest chain** (collapsible) with waypoints, status and **experience per quest** – straight from the server, also for new Forever quests.
- **Buy & craft:** vendor gear of your faction, the **auction house** (Auctionator prices, age of each price, gold limit) and recipes from CraftPilot – one click opens the recipe in CraftPilot, another its materials with your auction house prices.
- **Armor type** shown everywhere: a better leather piece beats your mail when its stats are better.
- **Slot filter** on every list.

## Dungeons

- **Every dungeon at a glance:** the ones in your own **dungeon range** first (set apart from your quest range), the others dimmed.
- **Dungeon details:** expected gain of one run, **every quest of the dungeon with its chain, experience and your best reward**, a waypoint to the entrance, "collect quests" for all pre-quests – and the **loot of every boss** (your upgrades bright, wishlist items marked).
- **Includes the new Forever dungeons:** The Drowned City, Krol'dok Stronghold, Alcaz Prison, Blackmaw Hold, The Shaper's Terrace, City of Dalaran and more – bosses, loot and quests, refreshed every week. Unknown drop chances are estimated from the boss's loot table and marked as estimated.
- **Dungeons to go:** your own list – order it, prepare all quests in one go (nearest first), see how often you have been there.

## Map

- **GearPilot's own map window:** quest givers of a chain (numbered), all quest givers of a zone, your dungeon preparation, your whole quest route – with your position.
- **Zoom** with the mouse wheel, **drag** to move, **hover** for quest and giver, **click** for a waypoint. One tab per zone.
- **Whole map** with all details, also in areas you have not explored yet – or **explored only**, if you want to discover them yourself.

## Wishlist and BIS

- **Wishlist** with every source of an item: quests with their chain, dungeon bosses with drop chance and entrance, vendors, mobs, crafting and the auction price – each with waypoint and map. Add items with one button or **Ctrl+Alt-click**.
- Chat hints when a wishlist item is in the auction house at your price, when your target or dungeon can drop one, and when you got it.
- **Calculated BIS lists** for your level range, pre-raid 60 or 60 with raids. **These are GearPilot's own calculation from your profile's weights – not an official BIS list.** Set bonuses and random effects are not scored.
- Export and import your list as text.

## Your character

- **Equipped gear** with the score of every slot and a **weapon setup** check: best two-hander against main hand + off hand.
- **Bags:** what is an upgrade and what can go – profession tools and CraftPilot's reserved materials are never listed.
- **Scoring for every role:** melee strength, melee agility, ranged, caster, healer and tank – picked from your talents (Survival hunters count as melee). Crit and hit count as percent; spell damage per school, mana per 5 and the other "Equip:" values are read from the tooltip. Your own weights or a **Pawn / WoWSims import** work too.
- **Strict proficiencies:** mail and plate only from level 40, shields, relics, weapon types and dual wield by class.

## Helpers outside the window

- **Item tooltip:** "x % better / worse" with a green or red arrow and a note when the weapon skill is missing or low.
- **Quest rewards:** the best reward is marked in the quest window – without an upgrade the one that sells for the most.
- **Need & greed:** one chat line per roll saying whether the item is an upgrade.
- **Names in your language:** GearPilot learns the names of the creatures you meet and shows them instead of the English database names.
- **Minimap button** and an entry in the addon compartment. Resizable window.

## Requirements

- **ForeverDB** – required, installed together with GearPilot by the CurseForge app. Quests, dungeon drops and vendors come from it; bag upgrades work without it.
- **Auctionator** – optional, for auction house prices.
- **TomTom** – optional, an arrow for every waypoint (otherwise the game's map waypoint is used).
- **CraftPilot** – optional, adds crafted gear, recipes and material costs, and respects its reserved materials.

Open with `/gpp` or the minimap button.

## Data

Quest, drop and vendor data: ForeverDB (GPL-3.0). Bosses, loot and quests of the new Forever dungeons: [AllTheThings](https://github.com/ATTWoWAddon/AllTheThings) (MIT), refreshed every week. Map details: WoW Forever client tables. Quest experience and item values come from your game. Source code: [GitHub](https://github.com/Necrite82/GearPilot) (GPL-3.0).

# GearPilot – dein Levelbegleiter für WoW Forever

**Welcher Gegenstand macht dich stärker, wo bekommst du ihn – und welche Quests lohnen sich?** GearPilot prüft Quest-Belohnungen, Dungeon-Beute, Händler, Auktionshaus, Handwerk und Taschen, bewertet jeden Gegenstand für deine Klasse und Talente, plant Quests und Dungeons und zeigt dir den Weg – auf einer eigenen Karte, mit mitlaufenden Wegpunkten.

- **Questroute:** die lohnendsten Quests deiner Stufe (EP plus Upgrade-Wert, auch am Ende von Questreihen) im lohnendsten Gebiet – annehmen, Ziele, abgeben, mit mitlaufenden Wegpunkten. Fokus Erfahrung, Ausrüstung oder ausgewogen; folgt deiner Stufe ins nächste Gebiet.
- **Beste Upgrades**, **Quests** (auch nach Gebiet, Questreihen aufklappbar, EP vom Server), **Kaufen & Herstellen** (Rezept und Materialkosten direkt in CraftPilot) und **Auktionshaus** mit Goldgrenze.
- **Dungeons:** alle auf einen Blick mit eigenem Dungeon-Bereich, Details mit allen Quests samt Vorquests und EP, der Beute jedes Bosses und dem Eingang – inklusive der neuen Forever-Dungeons. Dazu deine **„Dungeons to go“**-Liste.
- **Kartenfenster:** zoombar, ganze Karte oder nur Erkundetes, mit deiner Position und Wegpunkt per Klick.
- **Wunschliste** mit allen Quellen, Karte und Chat-Hinweisen, **berechnete BIS-Listen** – *GearPilots eigene Berechnung, keine offizielle BIS-Liste*.
- **Tooltip** mit „x % besser / schlechter“, Markierung der besten Quest-Belohnung, Hinweis beim Würfeln, NPC-Namen in deiner Sprache.
- Bewertung für Nahkampf, Fernkampf, Zauber, Heilung und Tank – automatisch nach Talenten, eigene Gewichte oder Pawn-Import; Rüstungsklasse überall sichtbar.
- **ForeverDB** wird benötigt (installiert die CurseForge-App mit), Auctionator, TomTom und CraftPilot sind optional.

Öffnen mit `/gpp` oder über den Minimap-Button. Fragen, Ideen und Fehler: [Discord](https://discord.gg/DpsBkHtQS).
