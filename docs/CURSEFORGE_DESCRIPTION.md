# GearPilot – gear upgrades while leveling in WoW Forever

**Which item makes you stronger – and where do you get it?** GearPilot checks your bags, quest rewards, dungeon drops, vendors, the auction house and crafted gear in your level range, scores every item for your class and talents and shows the best upgrades first, with waypoints to the quest giver, the dungeon entrance or the vendor.

GearPilot only reads and points the way – it never equips, buys or picks anything for you. German and English. Same look as **CraftPilot**, and both work together.

**Community, questions & bug reports:** [Discord](https://discord.gg/DpsBkHtQS) – no GitHub account needed.

## Upgrades

- **Best upgrades** from bags, quests and dungeons together, sorted by score gain – vendors, auction house and crafting can be added.
- **Quests** as items or **by zone** (where questing pays most), sorted by score, distance or zone. Dungeon quests are marked. The details show the **whole quest chain** with waypoints and the game's quest icons: done, in your log, next.
- **Dungeons** with boss and drop chance, or **per dungeon** with the expected gain of one run – every dungeon, the ones in your own dungeon range first. The dungeon details list every quest of the dungeon with its chain and your best reward, the entrance and the loot of every boss. **Dungeons to go:** your own list, with all pre-quests collected in one go. Includes the **new Forever dungeons** (The Drowned City, Krol'dok Stronghold, Alcaz Prison, Blackmaw Hold, The Shaper's Terrace, City of Dalaran and more). Unknown drop chances are estimated from the boss's loot table and marked as estimated.
- **Buy & craft:** vendor gear of your faction, the **auction house** (Auctionator prices, with the age of each price and a gold limit) and recipes from CraftPilot's catalog – sorted by score, price or score per gold.
- **Map window:** quest givers of a chain, a zone or your dungeon preparation on a zoomable map with numbered points, your position and one click for a waypoint.
- **Slot filter** on every list: only weapons, only rings, only chest …

## Wishlist and BIS

- **Wishlist** with every source of an item: quests with their chain, dungeon bosses with drop chance and entrance, vendors, mobs, crafting and the auction price. Add items with one button or **Ctrl+Alt-click**.
- Chat hints when a wishlist item is in the auction house at your price, when your target or dungeon can drop one, and when you got it.
- **Calculated BIS lists** for your level range, pre-raid 60 or 60 with raids. **These are GearPilot's own calculation from your profile's weights – not an official BIS list.** Set bonuses and random effects are not scored.
- Export and import your list as text.

## Your character

- **Equipped gear** with the score of every slot and a **weapon setup** check: best two-hander against main hand + off hand.
- **Bags:** what is an upgrade and what can go (CraftPilot's reserved materials are never listed).
- **Scoring for every role:** melee strength, melee agility, ranged, caster, healer and tank – picked automatically from your talents (Survival hunters count as melee). Spell damage per school, mana per 5 and the other "Equip:" values are read from the tooltip. Your own weights or a **Pawn / WoWSims import** work too.
- **Strict proficiencies:** mail and plate only from level 40, shields, relics, weapon types and dual wield by class.

## Helpers outside the window

- **Item tooltip:** "x % better / worse" with a green or red arrow and a note when the weapon skill is missing or low.
- **Quest rewards:** the best reward is marked in the quest window – without an upgrade the one that sells for the most.
- **Need & greed:** one chat line per roll saying whether the item is an upgrade.
- **Minimap button** and an entry in the addon compartment. Resizable window.

## Requirements

- **ForeverDB** – required, installed together with GearPilot by the CurseForge app. Quests, dungeon drops and vendors come from it; bag upgrades work without it.
- **Auctionator** – optional, for auction house prices.
- **TomTom** – optional, an arrow for every waypoint (otherwise the game's map waypoint is used).
- **CraftPilot** – optional, adds crafted gear and respects its reserved materials.

Open with `/gpp` or the minimap button.

## Data

Quest, drop and vendor data: ForeverDB (GPL-3.0). Bosses and loot of the new Forever dungeons: [AllTheThings](https://github.com/ATTWoWAddon/AllTheThings) (MIT), refreshed every week. Item values come from your game client. Source code: [GitHub](https://github.com/Necrite82/GearPilot) (GPL-3.0).

# GearPilot – Ausrüstungs-Upgrades beim Leveln in WoW Forever

**Welcher Gegenstand macht dich stärker – und wo bekommst du ihn?** GearPilot prüft Taschen, Quest-Belohnungen, Dungeon-Beute, Händler, Auktionshaus und Handwerk in deinem Levelbereich, bewertet jeden Gegenstand für deine Klasse und Talente und zeigt die besten Upgrades zuerst – mit Wegpunkt zum Questgeber, Dungeon-Eingang oder Händler.

- **Beste Upgrades**, **Quests** (auch nach Gebiet, mit ganzer Questreihe), **Dungeons** (pro Dungeon mit erwartetem Gewinn, allen Quests samt Vorquests und der Beute jedes Bosses, eigener Dungeon-Bereich, „Dungeons to go“-Liste), **Kaufen & Herstellen** und **Auktionshaus** mit Goldgrenze.
- **Kartenfenster** mit allen Questgebern einer Reihe, eines Gebiets oder deiner Dungeon-Vorbereitung – zoombar, mit deiner Position.
- **Wunschliste** mit allen Quellen und Chat-Hinweisen, **berechnete BIS-Listen** – *GearPilots eigene Berechnung, keine offizielle BIS-Liste*.
- **Tooltip** mit „x % besser / schlechter“, Markierung der besten Quest-Belohnung, Hinweis beim Würfeln.
- Bewertung für Nahkampf, Fernkampf, Zauber, Heilung und Tank – automatisch nach Talenten, eigene Gewichte oder Pawn-Import.
- **ForeverDB** wird benötigt (installiert die CurseForge-App mit), Auctionator, TomTom und CraftPilot sind optional.

Öffnen mit `/gpp` oder über den Minimap-Button. Fragen, Ideen und Fehler: [Discord](https://discord.gg/DpsBkHtQS).
